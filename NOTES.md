## Friend's heuristics

The friend — 300+ hours, every character cleared at Danger 5 — is this workspace's sole wisdom
source, and the reason the mission exists: the coached Danger 5 wins measure *their* model, not the
player's. Writing their heuristics down is how that model becomes transferable.

**Nothing has been captured yet.** The hooks below mirror the four research topics in
`RESOURCES.md`, so a heuristic can be dropped next to the mechanics it bears on.

Two rules for this section:

1. **Attribute by name.** A heuristic here is "the friend says X", never an anonymous fact.
2. **A heuristic is a hypothesis, not a source.** It gets tested against `RESOURCES.md` and against
   extracted game data. When one is disproved, that is a learning record, and it is exactly the kind
   of thing this project is built to surface.
