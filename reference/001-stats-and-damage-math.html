Stats and damage math

What a number on a stat screen actually does to a number on an enemy. Concept-level: nothing here is about a particular potato.

Brotato 1.1.12.0.beta-3 · 258 weapon entries (79 families × tiers), 247 items, 64 characters

The one sentence

A weapon has a base damage and a short list of stats it scales with, each with its own coefficient. Flat stats are added through those coefficients; % Damage multiplies what that sum produced; crit multiplies again. A stat not on the weapon's list does nothing for that weapon, whatever the stat screen implies.

1 · The order it computes in

  1. Base. The weapon's own damage at its tier.
  2. Flat scaling, added. For each stat on the weapon's scaling list, + (your stat × that weapon's coefficient). Several stats may be listed; all add into the same total. Interpretation STAT-S1, STAT-S2; coefficients extracted.
  3. % Damage, multiplied. Against the sum from 1–2, not the base alone. % Damage and % Explosion Damage stack additively with each other first. STAT-S1, STAT-S2, STAT-S3.
  4. Crit, multiplied. Per-weapon chance, per-weapon multiplier. There is no global crit-damage stat. STAT-S2, STAT-S11.
  5. Floor 1. Minimum damage from any weapon or effect. STAT-S1, STAT-S3, STAT-S13.

2 · Reading a scaling line

The tooltip's 20 (80% Melee) means: coefficient 0.8 on Melee Damage. The percentage is a literal multiplier on the stat, not on the damage.

Knife IV, the clean case — one scaling stat, nothing else. Base 25, coefficient 0.8. At 30 Melee Damage that is 25 + (30 × 0.8) = 49 a hit, before % Damage. Its crit is 50% for ×4, so 196 when it lands — both halves scale with tier, since Knife I is only 20% for ×2.5.

The trap: the melee/ranged label is cosmetic for scaling — a weapon scales with what its own list says, nothing else. Harpoon Gun IV is ranged and scales with both Ranged (1) and Melee (1). STAT-S2, STAT-S19; confirmed in extracted data.

4 · Coefficients decide what a stat is worth

A point of Ranged Damage is not a fixed amount of damage; it is worth whatever the weapon in your hands says. At tier IV the spread is a factor of twenty. Obliterator takes it at ×15 on a base of 400 and Laser Gun at ×6, while SMG takes 0.8 on a base of 8 and Minigun 0.75. Twenty Ranged Damage is +300 to the first and +15 to the last. The low-coefficient weapons are the fast ones, and the comparison only closes once you multiply by fire rate — §5, where the arithmetic stops being trustworthy.

5 · Attack Speed, and why the number lies

The rule of thumb is +100% Attack Speed halves the cooldown. STAT-S1, STAT-S4. The weapon's stored cooldown is in frames at 60 fps, so Sniper Gun IV's 120 frames is 2 s and Chainsaw IV's 2 frames is 0.03 s.

That second figure is plainly not 30 attacks per second, which is the point of this section: the cooldown is not the attack interval. Animation and recoil overhead sit on top, and at high Attack Speed they are nearly all of it. Datamined from ranged_weapon_stats.gd / melee_weapon_stats.gd, two independent sources: STAT-S5, STAT-S6. 

ranged: (cooldown / 60) + recoil_duration * 2
melee : (cooldown / 60) + recoil_duration + atk_duration / 2 + back_duration
  back_duration = 0.2 / (1 + AS * 3)
  atk_duration  = max(0.01, 0.2 - AS / 10) + range_factor * 0.15
  range_factor  = max(0, (max_range + Range / 2) / clamp(70 * (1 + AS / 3), 70, 120))

AS is a fraction: 1.0 is +100%. Read three things off it:

  • Recoil is a flat floor, and it varies per weapon. Most weapons sit at 0.1 s. The extremes: Chainsaw 0.01, Minigun and Flamethrower 0.02, SMG 0.05, and at the slow end Sniper Gun, Laser Gun and Obliterator 0.2. On a ranged weapon recoil is paid twice, so a 0.2 s recoil is a hard 0.4 s ceiling on fire rate no matter how much Attack Speed you buy.
  • Diminishing returns are per weapon, not one global curve. Weapons whose cooldown is already near zero — fast melee and fast ranged — are in diminishing returns from 0% Attack Speed onward, because the overhead terms already dominate. Fast ranged suffers more. STAT-S4.
  • Range slows melee down. range_factor puts weapon range and half your Range stat into the attack animation: roughly 3–10% attack speed per +100 Range. Attack Speed partially buys it back, saturating at +200%. STAT-S4, STAT-S5, STAT-S6, STAT-S12.

Known gap — do not compute DPS from the formulas above

None of these show Attack Speed applied to the cooldown / 60 term itself; that multiplier is unpublished (STAT-Q2) and the constants are from 2022–23 (STAT-Q7). Use them to rank weapons and to see why Attack Speed stops paying, never for an absolute number.

6 · Engineering and structures

  • Engineering is the only primary stat structures scale with. Turrets and landmines ignore Melee, Ranged, Elemental, % Damage and Attack Speed — but they do take secondary stats and item effects: piercing, bouncing, knockback, slow, explosion damage and size, burn, burn spread. STAT-S1, STAT-S8, STAT-S9, STAT-S10.
  • Same additive pattern as weapons: Turret 10 + (80% Eng) every 0.73 s, Landmines 10 (+100%), Laser Turret 20 (+125%), Explosive Turret 25 (+150%). Fire rates randomise to 70–130% per shot, so those intervals are averages. STAT-S8, STAT-S9, STAT-S10 — structure damage is not in the extracted weapon catalogue, so these four numbers are the only ones on this page carried on a source's word rather than the game's.
  • Structure Attack Speed is a separate stat from Attack Speed. Improved Tools converts 50% of your Attack Speed into it. STAT-S8, STAT-S9.
  • Structures cannot crit (unless you hold Pile of Books), and Incendiary Turret's burn scales with Engineering but not % Damage. STAT-S11, STAT-S14.
  • 21 weapon entries scale with Engineering too — Brick, Chain Gun, Chainsaw, DEX-troyer, Drill, Particle Accelerator, Plank, Screwdriver, War Hammer, the last at ×2, equal to its Melee coefficient.

7 · Crit

Both halves of a crit are properties of the weapon. Buying "crit" buys chance only, and what that chance is worth depends entirely on the multiplier attached to it. STAT-S2, STAT-S11.

Tier-IV weapon Chance Multiplier What it shows
Knife 50% ×4 Scales with tier: Knife I is 20% / ×2.5
Sniper Gun 20% ×4 Crit is most of its damage
Fist 1% ×1.5 Crit Chance is near-worthless here
Wand 0% — Five families cannot crit

Crits: every weapon hit and projectile, each pierce and each bounce independently, all explosions, item projectiles. Does not: structures, burn, randomly-targeted item damage. STAT-S11, STAT-S14.

9 · What this page does not know

STAT-Q2 — the Attack Speed cooldown expression is the big one: everything in §5 is overhead, and the multiplier on the cooldown timer itself is unpublished. STAT-Q1 — sources contradict each other on whether a 12-attacks-per-second cap exists; the datamined formulas show none. STAT-Q5 — rounding of fractional flat scaling is unknown; every worked example in the sources uses clean numbers.
